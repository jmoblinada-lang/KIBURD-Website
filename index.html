Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about keyboards, sizes, and features.

A mechanical keyboard uses individual switches beneath each key to provide a more responsive, precise, and satisfying typing experience. Different switches can also offer different feels and sounds.

The main difference is the number and arrangement of keys. 60% keyboards are compact and space-saving, 65% keyboards add arrow keys, 75% keyboards include more function keys, while Full Size keyboards include a complete layout with a dedicated number pad.

It depends on your needs and setup. 60% and 65% keyboards are great for compact desks and minimal setups, 75% offers a balance between functionality and size, while Full Size is ideal for users who frequently use the number pad and dedicated keys.
